# Rhythm Game Design Rules

## Purpose
Design a responsive 3D performance space where visuals support musical expression and moment-to-moment gameplay.

## Rules
1. Define the player experience and gameplay loop before designing decoration.
2. Establish the primary gameplay axis and a stable visual reference frame.
3. Keep important gameplay objects visually distinct from the background.
4. Make visual transitions correspond to intentional game or musical events.
5. Use minimalism as a deliberate composition choice, not as an excuse to leave the scene unfinished.
6. Ensure effects enhance anticipation, impact, or release rather than create constant noise.
7. Preserve consistent visual meanings for gameplay colors and symbols.
8. Make the environment support the song's emotional arc without obscuring notes or hazards.

## Design Brief
Before a major environment task, specify:
- Game mode and player viewpoint.
- Gameplay intensity and target response time.
- Song or music-section mood.
- Intended visual identity.
- Gameplay-critical colors and shapes.
- Target platform and comfort/performance requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria
The player can understand gameplay cues quickly, the environment has a recognizable identity, and visual effects feel coordinated rather than arbitrary.
